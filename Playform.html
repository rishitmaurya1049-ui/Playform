How PLAYFORM works

Start the PLAYFORM receiver on your PC before connecting. In the Android app, allow Nearby devices. Android 8–11 also need Location permission and the phone’s Location setting turned on for Bluetooth scans. If permission was denied, open Settings → Apps → PLAYFORM → Permissions and allow it. On the standalone website, connect using a browser with Web Bluetooth support.
